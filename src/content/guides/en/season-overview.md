## Overview

Season 6 changes the war format from **alliance versus alliance** to **faction versus faction**. In the story, a virus has poisoned the Great River, and two clans fight for control of the rainforest.

- **Season name:** Lost Rainforest
- **Map:** Shadow Rainforest Mega Map — one connected map with **8 warzones** and **1 Central Area**
- **Pre-season:** about 2 weeks, opened from the Season 6 event icon below the Duel VS button

## The two factions

| Faction | Emblem | Color |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Deepwood Clan** | 🦌 Deer | Green |
| **Wetland Clan** | 🐊 Crocodile | Blue |

Servers are assigned to factions in the **Faction Awards** event through the *Faction Reveal Stage*, which starts on day 1 of the pre-season. See the [Faction Reveal Tracker](../faction-reveal/) for the latest status and how it works.

Faction-related features:

- **Faction Technology** — research shared by the faction
- **Altars** and **Altar Conquest** — capture altars for buffs
- **Faction Duel** — faction-versus-faction duel
- **Influence Points** — faction points; capturing the Ancestral Temple grants a large amount

> One guide mentions a neutral NPC faction called the "Great River Clan". Other guides do not, so we treat this as unconfirmed.

## Map: Shadow Rainforest

- 8 warzones surround the **Central Area**.
- At the heart of the Central Area is the **Ancestral Temple**, the most prestigious target of the season.
- The 3×3 layout in the Faction Reveal Stage most likely mirrors each warzone’s position on the map.

## New resources & buildings

| Name | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Spore** | Main season resource. Used for Totems. |
| **Rainforest Mushroom** | Seasonal resource from the forest. |
| **War Merit** | Points from combat, tied to the *Extra War Merit* profession skill. |
| **Fungus Institute** | Seasonal building. |
| **Spore Factory** | Produces Spore. |
| **Totem** | Three totems that can be fed Spore. |

**Spore tip:** don’t split Spore evenly across all three Totems unless you run several equally competitive squads. Focus on the totem that supports your main squad.

## Professions

- Max level **100**.
- **No Diplomat** this season.
- **Engineer** is available. The second profession is not confirmed yet.
- New season skills include **Extra War Merit** and **Golden Bait**.

## Heroes

- **Hero Awakening** arrives in Season 6.
- **Braz** can be promoted from SSR to **UR**.
