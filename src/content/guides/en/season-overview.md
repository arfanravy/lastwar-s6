## Overview

Season 6 changes the war format from **alliance versus alliance** to **faction versus faction**. In the story, a virus has poisoned the Great River, and two clans fight for control of the rainforest.

| Topic | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Season name** | Lost Rainforest |
| **Map** | Shadow Rainforest Mega Map: one connected, mirror-symmetrical map with **8 warzones** and **1 Central Area** |
| **Length** | 2-week pre-season, then 8 weeks of season (week 8 is settlement only), then a Celebration of 4+ weeks |
| **Teams** | 4 warzones versus 4 warzones |
| **How a faction wins** | More **Influence Points** at the end of the season |

The pre-season is opened from the Season 6 event icon below the Duel VS button. Each server has its own countdown, and events start 10 to 30 minutes after it hits zero.

## Preparing before the season

From the MAAX alliance preparation guide:

1. **Alert Tower level 27.** Required for **Global Expedition**. Prioritise it before the season starts so you don't miss seasonal content.
2. **Hero Awakening.** Schedule: **Kimberly** in week 1, **DVA** in week 3, **Tesla** in week 5. Each hero needs **5 stars** and an **Exclusive Weapon at level 20 or higher**. Prepare them now so you can awaken them as soon as their week arrives.
3. **Rock the End.** Upgrade it as high as possible, up to **level 5**. Each level gives a **+0.5%** buff, for **+2.5%** in total at level 5.
4. **Braz to 5 stars.** Braz gets a **UR upgrade** in Season 6. Collect his shards now so you don't have to rush once the season starts.
5. **Resource stockpile (very important).** Recommended targets:

   | Resource | Target |
   | --- | --- |
   | Diamonds | 500K+ |
   | Universal Speed-ups | 300 days+ |
   | Advanced Teleporters (TP) | 100+ |
   | Training Speed-ups | as many as possible |
   | Healing Speed-ups | as many as possible |

   Don't spend these on anything unnecessary before the season starts.
6. **Enchanted Fungus base skin (mushroom).** A permanent skin, available through the **Event Market**. Save up if you want it, and keep an eye on seasonal events and their rewards.

> Preparation starts **before** the season, not after it begins. The better we prepare now, the stronger our position throughout Season 6.

## The three clans

| Clan | Emblem | Color | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Deepwood Clan** | 🦌 Stag | Green | Playable faction. Forest hunters, motto "The strong are the protectors". |
| **Wetland Clan** | 🐊 Crocodile | Blue | Playable faction. Swamp ambushers, motto "Survival is truth". |
| **Great River Clan** | — | — | Neutral NPC clan. Holds the Central Area, the Altars and the Ancestral Temple. Not playable. |

You **don't pick** your faction. The game splits the 8 warzones into two fixed groups of four, and the strongest warzone in each group becomes the **Faction Leader**. The faction belongs to the warzone and stays fixed for the whole season.

### Faction Awards (pre-season)

1. **Faction Selection Stage** (pre-season days 1–3): each Faction Leader's President makes a pick. They can change it with a 12-hour cooldown. If nobody picks, it is random. Only the President sees their own pick.
2. **Faction Reveal Stage** (from day 4 until the pre-season ends): everyone sees both final picks, the change history, and where each warzone lands on the map.
3. Warzones are placed on the map according to their number (No.1–No.4) inside the group.

See the [Faction Reveal Tracker](../faction-reveal/) for the live status of our group.

## Faction war: how fighting works

- **Destruction is permanent.** From week 3, capturing an enemy faction's city destroys it. It becomes a ruin with no owner, gives no adjacency, and cannot be rebuilt.
- **Influence.** The attacking alliance gains the city's Influence Points (higher level city, more points). The defending side loses it. If several alliances hit the same city, the one with the most Durability Damage gets the credit.
- **War days** are **Wednesday and Saturday**, with three battle windows each. Destroying and declaring war share a cap of **2 actions per war day**. Fishing Grounds don't use a declaration.
- **War Time schedule (in-game Rules screen):** uncaptured Fishing Grounds can be attacked every day. On Wednesday and Saturday, during the 3 Battle Times, captured Fishing Grounds and cities not under protection can be attacked too. **On Saturday, Commander bases can be attacked by other warzones** — activate a Shield if you are not fighting. Fishing Grounds in other warzones open from week 2, cities in enemy warzones from week 3. See the table in the [Calendar](../calendar/).
- **No protection after a defense.** The same city can be attacked again in the next window. Use **Alliance Safe Time** to lock one window (Alliance → Settings → Alliance Safe Time).
- **Same faction = no fighting** over Neutral Cities or Outposts.
- **Sanctuary**: each warzone's capital. Capturing your own gives the faction's top 60 alliances 200,000 Influence. If the enemy destroys it, that is taken back. Fought in the Sanctuary Duels (Saturdays of weeks 5–7).
- **Ancestral Temple**: decided in the Faction Duel. Three Giant Cannons surround it. The first faction to reach 100% gets **30 million Influence**; the loser gets nothing.

### Alliance Pacts

Two alliances of the same faction with adjacent territory can form a pact (not on Wednesday or Saturday). A pact shares adjacency, lets you reinforce each other's cities, shares war notifications, and opens a 200-member chat. Each alliance can have one pact at a time, and forming one starts a 3-day cooldown.

## Altars

Altars sit in the Great River Clan's Central Area. Each one turns on an **Alliance Skill** for the faction war.

| Level | Altar | Opens | Alliance Skill | Can't hold with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Snake Altar | Week 3 | Fortify (stronger structures) | Gust |
| 2 | Echo Altar | Week 3 | Mummy Army (summon) | Feather |
| 3 | Gust Altar | Week 4 | Warlord Missile | Snake |
| 4 | Feather Altar | Week 5 | Tesla Coil | Echo |
| 5 | Treehaven Altar | Week 6 | Refresh (resets another skill's cooldown) | — |

Rules:

- Altar Conquest runs **every Tuesday at 12:00 server time** for 1 hour.
- **No adjacency needed.** Enter the Altar's warzone and send a squad.
- Max **3 Altars** per alliance. Altars don't connect territory. The same skill twice doesn't stack.
- If nobody fully captures it in time, the previous owner keeps it.
- After capture, R4+ can summon the **Great River Clan's Gift** for members to claim.
- One source says holding an Altar needs about 1 billion power and more than 20 members.

## New resources & buildings

| Name | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Rainforest Mushroom** | Builds and upgrades **Spore Factories**. Your faction's cities also produce it hourly (War Spoils), but you must collect it by hand on the Faction Tech screen. Leftover Mushrooms become **Profession EXP** when the season ends. |
| **Spore** | Made by Spore Factories. Upgrades the **Fungus Institute** and the Totems. Leftover Spore becomes **Profession EXP** when the season ends. |
| **Fungus Institute** | Raises **Virus Resistance**. Low resistance makes strong squads underperform against seasonal bosses and defenders. Build it on day 1. |
| **Spore Factory (I–IV)** | Max level 30 each. Unlock all four first. The Fungus Institute and Spore Factories are removed after the season ends. |
| **Totems** | **Bear** (Tank), **Eagle** (Aircraft), **Jaguar** (Missile). Each boosts hero damage for one troop type. |
| **Protector's Field** | Summons Desert Protectors. |
| **War Merit** | Personal season score. Raises your Faction War Rank (19 ranks) for daily stipends and buffs. Spending it in the shop does **not** lower your rank total. |
| **Fishing Grounds** | Replace Strongholds. Your catch is donated to Alliance Skills and **Faction Technology**, and each donation gives War Merit. |

**Spore tip:** put all your Spore into the Totem for your main squad's troop type. Splitting it across three is a common mistake.

### Where War Merit comes from

First kills of Doom Elites and Rainforest Zombies, first captures of Fishing Grounds and Cities, Purge Action rankings, donating fish, reinforcing cities, Beneath the Ruins, and Faction Clash kill/defense rankings.

### Suggested priority

1. Spore Factories
2. Fungus Institute (Virus Resistance)
3. War Merit
4. Totem for your main troop type
5. Awakening for your main hero
6. Protector's Field
7. Braz UR promotion

## Professions

- Max level **100**.
- **No Diplomat** this season.
- **Engineer** is available. The second profession is not confirmed yet.
- New season skills include **Extra War Merit** and **Golden Bait**.
- Season skills turn off when the season ends and their points are refunded.

## Heroes

### Hero Awakening

Awakening is this season's hero feature. There are **no new Exclusive Weapons** in S6. Awakening swaps the hero's Expertise skill for a stronger Awakening skill and adds a lot of stats. Awakened heroes stay after the season.

| Hero | Type | Awakening opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Kimberly** | Tank | Week 1 (around day 4) |
| **DVA** | Aircraft | Week 3–4 |
| **Tesla** | Missile | Week 5–7 (varies by server) |

To awaken you need:

- the hero at **5 stars**
- their **Exclusive Weapon at level 20+**
- **50 named Awakening Shards** for that hero

Free shards come from the **Hero Awakening Trial** (up to 10 per hero) and **Global Expedition**. Universal Awakening Shards can raise Awakening Stars but cannot unlock Awakening.

| Milestone | Total shards |
| --- | --- |
| Unlock | 50 |
| Awakening Star 1 | 130 |
| Awakening Star 2 | 330 |
| Awakening Star 3 | 680 |
| Awakening Star 4 | 1,080 |
| Awakening Star 5 | 1,580 |

From around day 18 (week 6) an **Awakening Swap** lets you move stars, skill levels, gear and shards between two awakened heroes.

**Pick the hero that matches your main squad:** Tank → Kimberly, Aircraft → DVA, Missile → Tesla.

### Braz: SSR → UR

- The promotion window opens around **week 3**.
- Needs SSR Braz at **5 stars** plus **1 UR Hero Badge** (from the 500,000 total War Merit season goal).
- After promotion his level stays, but his **stars reset from 5 to 3** and his UR skills start at level 1. Getting back to 5 stars takes **1,600 SSR shards**.
- Skill Medals are mailed back. Eligible Wall of Honor shards come back as Braz shards.
- Braz, Kimberly, DVA and Tesla deal **+10% damage to monsters** during the season.
- UR Braz is built for PvP and faction fights, not monster farming. Get him to 5 stars during week 2.

### Tactics Cards

Tactics Cards return, but the old 4+1 hybrid card is gone. Each troop type now has its own card.

## What you keep when the season ends

**You keep:** your heroes, troops, HQ and research, Exclusive Weapons, Braz UR, awakened heroes, and Season Merit Medals.

**You lose:** the Fungus Institute, Spore Factories and Totems. Leftover Spore, Rainforest Mushroom, War Merit, bait, fish and Tactics Cards turn into **Profession EXP**, so spend them first.
